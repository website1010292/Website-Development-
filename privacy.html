Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy
Last updated: July 2026

MobileFix Pro ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it when you contact us about mobile repair, website development, or AI content services.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit — name, phone number, and email — through the contact form.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp when using our chat links.
  • Comments and ratings you choose to post on our contact page, stored locally in your browser.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and device, used only for improving site performance.

How we use your information

  • To respond to repair, web development, or content enquiries you submit.
  • To provide quotes, schedule services, and follow up on ongoing projects.
  • To improve our website, services, and customer support experience.

How comments work

Comments posted on our contact page are stored in your own browser's local storage so the page can display them back to you. We do not automatically transmit comment text to a server as part of this demo build; if you'd like comments centrally collected and moderated, connect the comment form to a backend of your choice.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited details with delivery or payment partners only where necessary to complete a service you've requested.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to delete any contact information we hold about you.
  • You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove comments saved on your device.
  • You can opt out of promotional messages at any time by telling us on WhatsApp.
