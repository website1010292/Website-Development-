WHAT WE BUILD

Ten kinds of websites. One clear process.

Every project starts with the same question: what does this website actually need to do? The answer decides which of these you need — and each one below explains what it is, who it's for, and exactly what you walk away with.

🎯

Landing Pages

What it is

A focused, single-page site built around one offer, one ad campaign, or one call to action — no menus, no distractions.

Who needs it

Businesses running Facebook/Google ads, launching a product, or promoting a single offer or event.

You receive

A fast-loading page with headline, offer details, a lead form or WhatsApp button, and mobile-first layout ready to link from ads.

📄

Static Websites

What it is

A fixed-content site with no database — hand-coded pages that load fast and rarely need updating.

Who needs it

Small businesses and service providers that need a simple, reliable information site.

You receive

A multi-page site (home, about, services, contact), fast load times, SEO-ready structure and a WhatsApp contact button.

🏢

Business Websites

What it is

A professional, multi-page site covering who you are, what you offer, and how to reach you.

Who needs it

Established businesses, agencies and service providers that need a credible online presence for search and referrals.

You receive

A 5–8 page site with services pages, an about page, a contact form, SEO-ready structure and 12 months of support.

🖼️

Portfolio Websites

What it is

A visual-first site that puts your work front and centre — project galleries, case studies and a clean bio page.

Who needs it

Designers, photographers, architects, freelancers and other creative professionals.

You receive

A project gallery, case-study pages, an about/bio section and an inquiry or contact form, styled around your visual work.

🦷

Dental & Clinic Websites

What it is

A specialised business website built for healthcare practices — services, doctor profiles and appointment requests.

Who needs it

Dentists, clinics, physiotherapists and private practices that want patients to find and trust them online.

You receive

Treatment/service pages, doctor & staff profiles, an appointment request form, location/map and a patient FAQ section.

🍽️

Restaurant Websites

What it is

A website built for food businesses — digital menu, ambience photos and ordering or reservation details.

Who needs it

Restaurants, cafés, cloud kitchens and catering businesses that want customers to browse and order easily.

You receive

A digital menu with pricing, a gallery, location & hours, and a WhatsApp ordering/reservation button on mobile-first pages.

🛒

E-commerce Websites

What it is

A full online store with product catalogues, cart, checkout and secure payment integration.

Who needs it

Retailers, boutiques and product-based businesses that want to sell directly online.

You receive

A product catalogue with categories, cart & checkout, payment gateway integration, and basic order management.

⚙️

Dynamic Websites

What it is

A database-driven site where content updates regularly — blogs, listings, directories or dashboards.

Who needs it

Businesses that publish content often, or need user accounts, search or filtering.

You receive

A simple admin panel to update content yourself, database-backed pages and search/filter functionality.

🧩

Custom Websites

What it is

A fully hand-coded build for functionality templates can't handle — booking systems, calculators, integrations.

Who needs it

Businesses with specific workflow or feature requirements that a standard website can't meet.

You receive

Custom-built features to your spec, integration with the tools you already use, and a tailored, scalable structure.

🔁

Website Redesign

What it is

A modern look, faster loading and cleaner structure for an existing site — without losing your content or rankings.

Who needs it

Businesses with an outdated site that no longer reflects their brand or performs well on mobile.

You receive

A refreshed design, improved load speed, full mobile optimisation, and your existing content migrated across.

FAQ

Common questions about our services

How long does a website project take? +

A landing page usually takes 3–5 days. A standard business website takes 7–10 days from content submission to launch. Online stores and custom builds typically take 3–5 weeks.

Do you build on WordPress or custom code? +

Sites are hand-coded rather than assembled from a page builder — which keeps the code lean, the site fast, and the design free of template constraints. A CMS is added when a project genuinely needs one.

Will my website work well on mobile phones? +

Yes — every website is designed and tested to be fully responsive, so it works correctly on desktop, tablet and mobile screens before it launches.

Can you redesign my existing website instead of starting over? +

Yes — redesigns keep your existing content and pages where possible, while modernising the look, speed and mobile experience.

Do you handle domain and hosting too? +

Yes — domain registration, hosting and email setup can all be handled as part of your project, or added afterwards.

Do you also offer mobile repair and AI content services? +

Yes, both are available alongside our web design and development work — see the "Also Available" section above for details.
